# Pokémon Gen 3 Distribution ROMs

Game Boy Advance ROMs from official Pokémon event distributions. They send
event Pokémon to Ruby, Sapphire, FireRed, LeafGreen and
Emerald over a **GBA link cable** and event tickets by **Wireless Adapter**, the way they were handed out at events and
in stores.

> **These are free.** If you paid for them, or for a cartridge with them on it,
> you've been scammed.

## What's included

## What you need

- **Two Game Boy Advance systems** (GBA, GBA SP or Game Boy Player) and a
  **GBA link cable**. The Nintendo DS and DS Lite have no link port, so they
  can't be used.
- **Two Wireless Adapters** to receive the event tickets!
- **A GBA flashcart** to run the distribution ROM on one of the systems.
- **Your game cartridge** in the other system.

Emulators with link cable support may work, but this hasn't been tested.

## How to use it (Event Pokémon only)

1. Turn both systems off and connect them with the link cable.
2. Start the distribution ROM on one system. For the event Pokémon ROMs, choose
   the Pokémon you want from the menu.
3. Start your game on the other system and follow the instructions on the
   distribution screen.

### Receiving tickets in your game

Mystery Gift has to be unlocked first. Answer the questionnaire in any Pokémon
Center with:

- **FireRed and LeafGreen**: **LINK TOGETHER WITH ALL**
- **Emerald**: **MYSTERY EVENT IS EXCITING**

1. Enable Mystery Gift.
2. Attach Wireless Adapter
3. Start searching for Wondercard.
4. Receive the Wondercard. Then collect the ticket in the game as the card describes. Show the
ticket to the sailor at the harbor (Vermilion City in FireRed and LeafGreen,
Lilycove or Slateport City in Emerald) to set sail.

## Troubleshooting

- **Nothing happens.** Check the link cable is pushed fully into both systems,
  and that the ROM's region matches your game's.
- **The game says it can't receive the ticket.** Check that the ROM supports
  your game and that Mystery Gift is unlocked.
